# Perfect Sound privacy policy

*Last updated: 4 October 2026*

Perfect Sound is a free, open-source music player for Android made by
[sanjaynathwani-blip](https://github.com/sanjaynathwani-blip) and published on Google Play as
**Bookmatched Apps**. This policy covers the app as published on Google Play and on
[GitHub](https://github.com/sanjaynathwani-blip/perfect-sound).

## What Perfect Sound collects

**Nothing.** Perfect Sound has no internet permission, so it can't send anything off your
device. It has no accounts, analytics, crash reporting, advertising or advertising ID, and it
doesn't share data with anyone.

## What stays on your device

To work, Perfect Sound keeps a few things in its own private storage on your device:

- your playlist, as links to the files and folders you added, and where you left off;
- settings such as the window layout, visualizer mode and colour theme.

Uninstalling Perfect Sound, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes all of it.

## Permissions

Every permission is optional and asked for only when you use the feature that needs it.

| Permission | What it's used for |
| --- | --- |
| Files you pick or drop | Playing the files and folders you choose. Perfect Sound sees nothing else of your storage. |
| Notifications | The media controls while music plays in the background. |
| Notification access | Android's way of letting an app see and control Chrome's media sessions (title, artist, art, play/pause). Perfect Sound also reads the site name in Chrome's media notification, to tell the Spotify and YouTube Music web players from other tabs. It isn't used to read any other notifications. |
| Record audio, and Android's share prompt | Hearing other apps' playback so the visualizer can move to it in remote mode. Only media playback is captured, never the microphone, and the sound is analysed on the device as it plays: nothing is recorded, kept or sent. |

## Children

Perfect Sound isn't directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
